# Tahee 🌸🪡
### **Problem Statement**
Localized Filipino tailors (_mananahi_) struggle with informal order tracking and disorganized pickup schedules, often resulting in poor communication with the customers, missed deadlines, and lost revenue from unmanaged rush requests. Without a dedicated tracking system, tailors frequently lose visibility over order completion timelines and daily business performance. 

---

### **Target Audience**
Currently, most local Filipino tailors cope with the issue by relying on manual processes, including hand-written notebooks, receipt slips, memorization, and verbal agreements with clients.

- Local Filipino tailors (mananahi): This will benefit them because it will ease their work in regard to improved tracking of orders, efficient scheduling, and proper organization. It will eliminate mistakes, deadlines missing, and loss of money.

- Tailors’ customers: Clients will experience more reliable communication, timely completion of orders, and fewer mistakes or lost garments. This leads to higher customer satisfaction and loyalty.

---

### **Proposed Solution**
Tahee is a progressive web application that streamlines order management for local tailors (_mananahi_) by providing real-time progress tracking, automated pickup reminders, and intelligent deadline alerts. It professionalizes tailor-client communication with transparent status updates and automated rush-fee calculations, helping tailors eliminate delivery delays and maximize shop earnings.

Tahee introduces them to a digital system that allows tailors to manage their orders digitally. Mainly supporting SDG 8, 9, 12.
- **SDG 8:**
		Tahee promotes SDG 8 as local tailors are losing customers and income, our
	app helps them keep customers through better tracking and communication.
- 	**SDG 9:**
		The app brings digital tools to MSME’s that still rely on pen and papers.
 - **SGD 12:**
		Consumers choosing to alter, tailor, and repair their clothes equates to them
	buying and wasting less.


---

## **Output**
: Progressive Web App (PWA) 

## ✨ Features
### 👤 Customer's End

- **Local Tailor Discovery** – Displays nearby tailoring businesses based on the customer's location.
- **Service Selection** – Allows customers to browse and select available tailoring services.
- **Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA)** – Shows the estimated completion date and time of the customer's order.
- **Rush Order Option** – Allows customers to request faster service for an additional fee, subject to the tailor's availability.

---

### 🧵 Seller's End

- **Business Dashboard** – Displays the tailor's available services, products, and active orders.
- **Business Location** – Allows sellers to enter their shop address so customers can find their business.
- **Business Profile Setup** – Allows sellers to create a profile containing their business name, description, services, and other relevant details.
- **Customer Reviews** – Displays feedback and ratings from previous customers to help build trust and credibility.

---

### 🌐 Web Application Features

- **User Role Selection** – Allows users to choose whether they are accessing Tahee as a customer or a tailor.
- **Report Feature** – Allows both customers and sellers to report suspicious activity, inappropriate behavior, or order-related issues.
